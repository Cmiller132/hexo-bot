lab — the live model, on your position

Build any position on the board. Then inspect what the real network computes on it: the input features, the evaluation, the move preferences. The heads module shows the policy and the critic cell by cell. The other modules show the attention pattern from any cell, the activity in the trunk, a real (budget-capped) search, and the forced-win solver. Everything on the right reads the position on the left. In legal-sequence mode the bot can also play its own moves. Step back and play a different move to start a new line; the old line stays on the strip. Positions from the analysis tab open here with their ?game=…&ply=… link.
